How Does Endermologie® Work?

LPG® Endermologie® uses patented Independent Motorized Roller (I.M.R.) and Motorized Pulsating Flap (M.P.F.) technologies to create a unique cellular stimulation through reproducible mechanical massage.

The treatment uses an aspiration system that gently embraces a skinfold to stimulate tissues of the body and face, targeting connective tissues including skin, muscle, and circulatory systems.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LPG® Endermologie treatments

What is LPG® Endermologie?

Endermologie is an effective, safe, natural, and FDA-cleared technique for cellular stimulation via mechanical massage. It uses patented Independent Motorized Roller (I.M.R.) and Motorized Pulsating Flap (M.P.F.) technologies to target connective tissues and improve skin appearance.

Is Endermologie safe and comfortable?

Yes, absolutely! Most clients describe it as feeling like a relaxing massage with no downtime required. The treatment intensity can be adjusted for individual comfort, and there are no known side effects.

How long do treatments take?

Body treatments typically last 35-45 minutes, while facial treatments take 20-25 minutes. Targeted area sessions can be as short as 15-20 minutes.

When will I see results?

Many clients notice immediate skin improvements after their first session. Significant results typically appear after 6-8 sessions, with continued benefits through monthly maintenance sessions.

What benefits can I expect?

LPG® Endermologie reduces cellulite appearance, improves body contour, enhances skin elasticity, boosts circulation, and provides deep relaxation. It's the world's first FDA-cleared cellulite treatment.

Who can receive Endermologie treatments?

Both men and women can benefit from Endermologie. It's perfect for addressing cellulite, body contouring, skin firmness, circulation improvement, and post-surgical recovery.

How does Endermologie compare to other treatments?

Unlike CoolSculpting or other aggressive procedures, Endermologie is non-invasive with no side effects, treats the entire body including face, and specifically targets cellulite while feeling like a relaxing massage.

When should I start treatments for summer?

Start early! Initial results appear in 2-3 weeks, with full results in 8 weeks. Beginning treatments in spring ensures you'll feel confident and ready for summer activities.
